## Scoring Workflow Per Session

1. Use the matching track scorecard.
2. Record per-round score and reasons.
3. List top 2 strengths and top 2 gaps.
4. Define 3 targeted drills before next session.
5. Update [master-evaluator.md](master-evaluator.md) with the session average.

## Graduation Targets by End of Week 16

- At least 2 sessions with overall score >= 8.5
- No single round below 7 in final week
- Ability to clearly explain trade-offs under time pressure
